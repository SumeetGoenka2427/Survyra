## Survyra Unique Strengths

1. **Reputation Management Engine** – Post-survey routing to Google/Facebook/WhatsApp/complaint form with click tracking. No competitor does this out-of-the-box for SMBs.
2. **Multi-client SaaS architecture** – Admin manages multiple client accounts with subscription plans.
3. **19 question types with multiple visual styles** – More style variety than most competitors.
4. **5 survey layouts** – Multi-step, one-page, card-based, section-wizard, conversational.
5. **Sentiment-driven thank-you rules** – Automatic score bucketing with configurable actions per sentiment.
6. **Encrypted contact data** – Phone/email encrypted at rest.
7. **WhatsApp campaign support** – Rare among survey platforms.
