# Phase 4 (Days 11–17) — Aggressive Outreach

Now you start selling.

---

## Daily Minimum

- [ ] 20–30 new businesses contacted per day → **140–210 prospects/week**
- [ ] Personalize at least the first sentence of every message — don't spam

## Daily Schedule

- **Morning (1 hr)** — find 20–30 businesses
- **Afternoon (1–2 hrs)** — send Instagram DM / WhatsApp / email where appropriate
- **Evening (1 hr)** — follow up on previous contacts

## The Funnel (expect this ratio, don't panic if early days look worse)

```
30 Prospects → 10 Replies → 5 Conversations → 3 Demos → 1 Trial
```

Don't expect every prospect to convert — you're building volume, not chasing a single close.

## Follow-Up Discipline (from [phase-3-sales-machine.md](phase-3-sales-machine.md))

- [ ] Every contacted prospect gets Follow-up #1 after 2 days if silent
- [ ] Every still-silent prospect gets Follow-up #2 after another 3–4 days
- [ ] Then **stop** — no third message

## Day-by-Day (Days 11–17)

- [ ] Day 11 — 25 prospects, 20+ DMs sent
- [ ] Day 12 — 25 prospects, 20+ DMs sent, follow-ups on Day 11's non-replies queued for Day 13
- [ ] Day 13 — 25 prospects, 20+ DMs sent, Day 11 follow-ups sent
- [ ] Day 14 — 25 prospects, 20+ DMs sent, Day 12 follow-ups sent
- [ ] Day 15 — 25 prospects, 20+ DMs sent
- [ ] Day 16 — 25 prospects, 20+ DMs sent, week's follow-up #2 batch sent
- [ ] Day 17 — 25 prospects, 20+ DMs sent; end-of-week funnel numbers logged in [execution-tracker.md](execution-tracker.md)

Log every day's numbers in the daily scoreboard in the tracker — that's what tells you where the funnel is actually leaking (not enough prospects? low reply rate? demos not converting?).

### Phase 4 exit checklist
- [ ] 150–200+ prospects contacted across the week
- [ ] Follow-up cadence maintained without exception
- [ ] Daily scoreboard filled in every day, not retroactively
