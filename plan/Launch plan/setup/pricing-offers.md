# Plans, Launch Offer & Early-Bird Offer

Expands the single Growth Package from [00-overview.md](../00-overview.md) into a full pricing sheet, plus the launch-specific offers used to convert the first trials into paying clients (see [phase-5-first-clients.md](../phase-5-first-clients.md)).

**Rule reminder (from [00-overview.md](../00-overview.md)):** never promise unlimited work at any tier. Every plan below includes the same clause: *"Website setup + basic monthly updates included. Major redesigns, additional functionality and custom development are separately quoted."*

## 2. Launch Offer (first 30 days, limited slots)

Purpose: convert early trials into signed clients faster by making "now" better than "later." Use this only during the active 30-day sprint — don't run it indefinitely or it becomes the new baseline price.

> **Founding Client Offer — First 10 businesses only**
> Growth Package at ₹2,000/month for the first 3 months (instead of ₹3,000), then standard pricing applies.
> Setup fee waived (normally ₹1,500 one-time).

Framing line for DMs/demos:
> "Since we're onboarding our first businesses this month, I can get you set up on the Growth Package at ₹2,000/month for the first 3 months, no setup fee — that's only open for the first 10 businesses we bring on."

Rules:
- [ ] Track the "X/10 slots filled" count somewhere visible to you (and honestly — don't fake scarcity if you're client #2)
- [ ] Offer expires either at 10 signups or Day 30, whichever comes first
- [ ] After the offer ends, new clients go straight to standard pricing — existing founding clients keep their locked-in first-3-months rate as agreed

## 3. Early-Bird / Trial-to-Paid Offer

Purpose: the specific nudge used at the end of the 7–14 day trial (from [phase-5-first-clients.md](../phase-5-first-clients.md)) to convert a trial into a paid signup without discounting forever.

> "Your trial's been running for [X] days and you've already gotten [Y] responses / [Z] reviews. If you lock in this week, I'll keep your first month at ₹1,500 instead of ₹3,000, and lock in the Founding Client rate after that — otherwise trial access ends on [date]."

Mechanics:
- [ ] Trial has a hard end date, stated upfront on Day 1 of the trial (never open-ended — Rule 4 in [00-overview.md](../00-overview.md))
- [ ] Conversion nudge sent 2–3 days before trial end, not on the last day
- [ ] Discount is time-boxed to "convert this week" — creates urgency without being pushy, and ties back to real results the trial already produced (use their actual response/review numbers, not generic claims)
