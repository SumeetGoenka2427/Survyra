# Branding Guide

Covers Day 2 of [phase-1-foundation.md](../phase-1-foundation.md) in full. Everything downstream (Instagram profile, posts, reels, demo decks, pricing sheet) should pull from this file so the brand is consistent from post #1.

---

## 1. Positioning (locked in [00-overview.md](../00-overview.md))

> **We help small businesses get more customers through modern websites, customer feedback and review growth.**

Title yourself **Customer Growth & Digital Experience** — never "website developer" or "freelancer."

## 2. Name & Tagline

- **Name: Survyra** (locked — see [instagram-setup.md](instagram-setup.md) → Naming Decision. One unified brand across the website, survey/feedback, and review-growth offer — no separate agency name.)
- **Tagline (locked): "Turn Customers Into Growth"** — short enough for a logo lockup and the Instagram name field (`Survyra | Turn Customers Into Growth` won't fit the name field character limit, so use it in the bio/highlight cover instead, not appended to the username).
- Backup taglines (use as alternate captions/headlines when the primary gets repetitive, not as a replacement):
  - "More Customers. More Reviews. Less Hassle." (use for pricing/offer posts — most outcome-explicit)
  - "Websites, Feedback, Reviews — Simplified" (use for the "Who We Are" intro post)
  - "Local Business Growth, Made Simple" (use for reel captions)

## 3. Voice & Tone

- **Talk like a growth partner, not a vendor.** Lead with outcomes ("more reviews," "more customers"), not features ("survey builder," "QR generator").
- **Short sentences. No jargon.** Your buyers are salon/restaurant/clinic owners, not technical people — never say "multi-tenant," "API," "dashboard analytics module."
- **Confident, not salesy.** State what you do plainly; avoid exclamation-mark-heavy hype copy.
- **Always end with a next step.** Every post, story, and DM should point somewhere (DM "DEMO", link in bio, WhatsApp).

## 4. Color Palette (locked)

| Role | Color | Hex | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Deep Indigo-Blue | `#1E3A5F` | Trust/professionalism — dominant color on the wordmark, website demos, and pricing sheet |
| Accent | Warm Gold/Amber | `#F2A93B` | CTAs, "DEMO" callouts, checkmarks — doubles as a nod to 5-star reviews, which is the core outcome being sold |
| Neutral (light) | Off-white | `#F7F5F0` | Default background for posts/light-mode assets |
| Neutral (dark) | Near-black | `#14161A` | Reel covers and any dark-mode asset — never mix light and dark neutrals in the same single asset |

Use exactly these four values everywhere — don't introduce a 5th color. Consistency across post, reel cover, and highlight icon is what makes the profile read as "a small SaaS/growth company" rather than a scrapbook.

## 5. Typography (locked)

- **Display/headlines/reel text: Poppins** (bold weight) — geometric, modern, reads well at small sizes on mobile
- **Body/captions/longer text: Inter** (regular/medium weight) — clean, highly legible, free via Google Fonts
- Never introduce a 3rd font anywhere client-facing (demo sites, posts, pricing sheet, WhatsApp catalog)

## 6. Logo Direction (locked)

- **Phase 1 (Month 1): wordmark-only.** "Survyra" set in Poppins Bold, Primary (`#1E3A5F`) on transparent background — no icon yet. Faster to produce, and avoids committing to an icon before the brand has run in the market.
- **Phase 2 (once 5+ clients, revisit per [00-overview.md](../00-overview.md) → multi-tenant timing): add a small upward-arrow icon** next to the wordmark. Meaning: growth/upward trend — ties directly to the "more customers, more reviews" outcome, and is simple enough to double as a standalone app icon later.
- Must work as a small circular profile photo (test at 110×110px, Instagram's profile-photo size) and still be legible.
- Keep a transparent-background PNG version for demo decks and the pricing sheet.
- Exact generation prompts for both phases are in [content-prompts.md](content-prompts.md) → Logo Generation Prompts.

## 8. Imagery Style

- Real screenshots of your actual demo sites/survey/dashboard — not generic stock photography
- Before/After framing wherever possible (old website vs. new, no feedback system vs. dashboard) — this is your strongest visual hook per the Reels in [phase-2-instagram-presence.md](../phase-2-instagram-presence.md)
- Consistent border/frame treatment on screenshots (e.g. always inside a browser-chrome or phone-frame mockup) so the feed looks like one system, not a scrapbook
