# Phase 3 (Days 8–10) — Build the Sales Machine

This is where the aggressive part starts.

---

## Day 8 — Build the Prospect List

- [ ] Create spreadsheet with columns: `Business | Category | City | Instagram | Website | Reviews | Phone | WhatsApp | Contacted | Reply | Demo | Trial | Paid`
- [ ] Source **100 prospects**
- [ ] Prioritize 2–3 categories first: **Salons + Restaurants + Clinics** (gyms, cafés, hotels, coaching centers, local services are the wider pool once these are dialed in)

Use the same spreadsheet as the master prospect tracker referenced in [execution-tracker.md](execution-tracker.md).

## Day 9 — Build the Sales Script

Don't sell software — sell the result.

- [ ] First message drafted and ready to paste:
  > Hi, I came across your business and noticed you have good customer activity online.
  >
  > We help local businesses collect customer feedback and turn happy customers into more reviews using a simple survey/QR system.
  >
  > We also provide a modern business website as part of the monthly plan.
  >
  > I've created a small demo showing how this could work for your business. Would you like me to share it?
- [ ] Follow-up #1 (send after 2 days of silence) drafted:
  > Hi, just following up on my previous message. I can show you the demo in 5 minutes if you'd like to see how the feedback + review system works.
- [ ] Follow-up #2 (send after another 3–4 days) drafted:
  > We recently prepared a simple setup for local businesses to collect customer feedback and improve their review flow. Happy to show you the demo if this is something you're exploring.
- [ ] Rule locked in: after follow-up #2, **stop**. No further messages.

## Day 10 — Prepare the Demo Flow

Single demo flow, no technical explanation:

- [ ] 1. Business website
- [ ] 2. Customer scans QR
- [ ] 3. Survey
- [ ] 4. Rating
- [ ] 5. Positive/negative branch
- [ ] 6. Review request
- [ ] 7. Business dashboard
- [ ] 8. Monthly report
- [ ] 9. Pricing

### Phase 3 exit checklist
- [ ] 100-row prospect list ready, prioritized by category
- [ ] Opening message + 2 follow-ups scripted and saved somewhere copy-pasteable
- [ ] Demo walkthrough rehearsed against the 9-step flow above
