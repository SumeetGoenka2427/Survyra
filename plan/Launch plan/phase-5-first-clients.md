# Phase 5 (Days 18–21) — Get Your First Clients

By now you should have conversations, demos, objections, interested people, and possibly trials already in motion. Focus heavily on conversion.

---

## Offer a Trial, Not Free Work

- [ ] Trial length fixed at **7–14 days** (never open-ended)
- [ ] Trial includes: website demo, survey, QR, dashboard, review funnel
- [ ] Framing line ready: *"Let's run this with your customers for 7–14 days and see what feedback you receive."*

## First Customer Onboarding Checklist

Collect from the client:

- [ ] Business name
- [ ] Logo
- [ ] Phone
- [ ] WhatsApp
- [ ] Address
- [ ] Services
- [ ] Images
- [ ] Social links
- [ ] Google review link
- [ ] Domain
- [ ] Survey questions
- [ ] Brand colors

## Onboarding → Launch Flow

```
Client → Content → Website → Survey → QR → Review Funnel → Launch
```

- [ ] Launch within **48–72 hours** of the client saying yes (Rule 5 from the overview)
- [ ] Every custom request the client makes gets written into the SaaS Requirements backlog in [execution-tracker.md](execution-tracker.md) — don't build it ad hoc, don't build it for free

### Phase 5 exit checklist
- [ ] At least one trial actively running (ideally 2–5, per the funnel math in [phase-4-outreach.md](phase-4-outreach.md))
- [ ] Onboarding checklist completed for every client that said yes
- [ ] Each client launched within 48–72 hours of signing on
