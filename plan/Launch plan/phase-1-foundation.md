# Phase 1 (Days 1–3) — Build the Foundation

Don't touch multi-tenancy yet. Your existing products (2 websites + Survyra) are enough to start selling.

---

## Day 1 — Decide the Business

- [ ] Lock the positioning statement: *"We help small businesses get more customers through modern websites, customer feedback and review growth."*
- [ ] Confirm the 3 services you're selling: 🌐 Website · 📊 Customer Feedback · ⭐ Review Growth
- [ ] Drop "website developer" from any bio/description — use **Customer Growth & Digital Experience**
- [ ] Lock the Growth Package offer at **₹3,000/month** (see [00-overview.md](00-overview.md) for the full scope)
- [ ] Write the "no unlimited work" clause into the offer sheet: *"Website setup + basic monthly updates included. Major redesigns, additional functionality and custom development are separately quoted."*
