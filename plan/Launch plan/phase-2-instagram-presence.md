# Phase 2 (Days 4–7) — Build Instagram Presence

You don't need 100 posts. You need a profile that looks legitimate the moment a prospect checks you out.

---

## Day 4 — Instagram Setup

- [ ] Bio written, e.g.:
  ```
  🌐 Websites for Small Businesses
  📊 Customer Surveys & Feedback
  ⭐ Turn Happy Customers Into Reviews
  🚀 Simple Growth Tools
  👇 DM "DEMO"
  ```
- [ ] CTA is unmissable: **DM "DEMO"**
- [ ] Links added: WhatsApp, website, portfolio, contact

## Day 5 — First 5 Posts

- [ ] Post 1 — Who We Are: *"Helping small businesses build a better online presence and understand their customers."*
- [ ] Post 2 — Website Showcase (strongest website)
- [ ] Post 3 — Customer Feedback: *"Your customers are already giving you feedback. Are you listening?"* + survey screenshot
- [ ] Post 4 — Review Funnel diagram: Customer → Survey → Happy Customer → Review
- [ ] Post 5 — Offer: *"Website + Customer Feedback + Review Growth"* + **DM "DEMO"**

## Day 6 — Create 3 Reels (10–20 seconds each)

- [ ] Reel 1 — "Your website could be losing customers." (Before → After)
- [ ] Reel 2 — "This QR code can collect customer feedback in seconds." (Scan → Survey → Dashboard)
- [ ] Reel 3 — "What happens after a customer gives 5 stars?" (Survey → Review Request)

## Day 7 — Finish Profile

- [ ] Highlights created: Services / Websites / Surveys / Reviews / Demos / Pricing / About
- [ ] Positioning test run: ask 3–5 trusted people *"If you opened this Instagram account for the first time, what do you think we sell?"* — if they can't answer immediately, fix the positioning before moving on

### Phase 2 exit checklist
- [ ] Profile bio + CTA + highlights live
- [ ] 5 posts + 3 reels published
- [ ] Positioning validated by outside eyes
